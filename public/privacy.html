This page explains what data nxgp.io collects and how it is used. It is written to be read, not to be skimmed past — if anything is unclear, email hello@nxgp.io.

The Nx Assistant (chat)

Our website assistant is an AI. When you use it:

  • Conversations are stored. What you type and the assistant's replies are saved so we can respond to inquiries and improve the assistant.
  • Contact details you volunteer (name, email, company, what you need) are passed to our team so we can follow up — that's the point of sharing them.
  • Messages are processed by Anthropic (the AI provider) to generate replies, and our team is notified via Slack when you ask to be contacted.
  • Improvement uses patterns, not identities. When we analyze conversations to make the assistant better, contact details are stripped first and only generalized patterns are kept ("visitors often ask about X"), never who asked.
  • Retention: raw chat transcripts are deleted after 90 days. If you shared contact details as a business inquiry, we keep those to manage the relationship.
  • Your browser stores the current conversation locally (so it survives a page reload). Clearing site data removes it.

What we don't do

  • No advertising trackers, no analytics cookies, no fingerprinting.
  • We do not sell or rent your information to anyone.
  • We do not use your conversations to train AI models — only to look up patterns as described above.

Technical basics

The site is hosted on Vercel; conversation data and inquiries are stored with Supabase. IP addresses are used transiently for rate limiting (abuse prevention). If you book a call, scheduling is handled by Calendly under its own privacy terms.

Your choices

Email hello@nxgp.io to ask what we hold about you, or to have your conversations and contact details deleted. We'll act on it promptly.
